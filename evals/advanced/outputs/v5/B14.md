## Which deadlines apply?

Delta's and Foxtrot's assessments were published on September 4, so the new policy applies. Excluding publication day, the five working days are:

1. Monday, September 7.
2. Tuesday, September 8.
3. Wednesday, September 9.
4. Friday, September 11.
5. Monday, September 14.

Thursday, September 10 is a declared holiday and does not count; weekends also do not count. Their ordinary deadline is therefore **September 14 at 17:00 local time**, equivalent to **15:00 UTC**. [B14-S1; B14-S3]

Echo's assessment was published on August 31, so the earlier policy applies. Its seven calendar days are September 1 through September 7, including the weekend. Echo's deadline is **September 7 at 17:00 local time**, also **15:00 UTC**. Filing in September does not change which policy governs an August assessment. [B14-S2; B14-S3]
