An **opportunity cost** is the value of the best alternative you give up when you choose something. Money you've already spent and cannot recover is a **sunk cost**.

Suppose you already bought a nonrefundable $15 movie ticket. Tonight, you can either attend the movie or work a short shift earning $40.

- The **$15 ticket price is a sunk cost**: you cannot get it back whichever option you choose.
- If working is your best alternative, the **opportunity cost of attending the movie is the $40 you could have earned**.

Opportunity cost can also involve time, rest, or enjoyment. It concerns what your choice makes you give up now, and it counts the best alternative—not the sum of every possible alternative.
