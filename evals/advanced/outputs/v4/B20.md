### Calculate the threshold before applying the exception

The ordinary rule concerns physical loans from network museums to outside institutions. Review is required when the relevant insured value **exceeds 50,000 units**; exactly 50,000 would not exceed the threshold. Related loans departing within any thirty-day interval are added together. “Related” means the same exhibition agreement, not simply the same borrower. [B20-S1]

Ash and Beech share the River Rooms agreement and depart only eight days apart:

\[
30{,}000 + 25{,}000 = 55{,}000\text{ units}.
\]

They therefore cross the ordinary threshold together. Splitting those departures across dates does not avoid aggregation. The thirty-day rule also crosses calendar-month boundaries. [B20-S1, B20-S3]
