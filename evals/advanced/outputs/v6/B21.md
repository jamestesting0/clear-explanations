**Recurring revenue from the opening customers grew, even though six of those customers left.** Net recurring-revenue retention exceeds 100% because expansion by four Enterprise accounts more than offsets the revenue lost through cancellations. It measures retained and expanded contract value, not the proportion of customers who stayed. [B21-S1; B21-S2; B21-S4]

### What closed the month

The **opening cohort** means the same 65 accounts present at the start. New customers are counted separately, and a plan change does not create a new customer identity. [B21-S1]

| Closing group | Accounts | Contracted monthly recurring revenue |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Surviving opening Starter accounts | 45 | 45 × 20 = 900 units/month |
| Surviving opening Pro accounts | 9 | 9 × 100 = 900 units/month |
| Surviving opening Enterprise accounts | 4 | 6,000 units/month |
| **Opening cohort still active** | **59** | **7,800 units/month** |
| New Starter and Pro accounts | 20 + 2 = 22 | 20 × 20 + 2 × 100 = 600 units/month |
| **All closing accounts** | **81** | **8,400 units/month** |

These totals follow from [B21-S2; B21-S3]. The account count also reconciles as **65 opening − 6 canceled + 22 acquired = 81 closing**.

### Three retention measures answer three questions

| Measure | Calculation | What it tells the manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account retention | 59 ÷ 65 ≈ **90.77%** | The share of opening accounts still active |
| Net recurring-revenue retention | 7,800 ÷ 7,000 ≈ **111.43%** | Closing monthly contract value from opening accounts, including expansion, relative to their opening value |
| Gross recurring-revenue retention | 5,800 ÷ 7,000 ≈ **82.86%** | Opening monthly contract value retained, excluding expansion above each survivor's opening amount |

Calculations use the definitions in [B21-S4] and account data in [B21-S1; B21-S2]. Percentages are rounded to two decimal places.

For gross retention, count the four surviving Enterprise accounts at their original combined **4,000 units/month**, rather than their expanded 6,000. Add the surviving Starter and Pro amounts: **900 + 900 + 4,000 = 5,800 units/month**. No survivor reduced its recurring contract amount, so the entire difference from the opening 7,000 comes from canceled accounts. [B21-S2]

The six cancellations comprise five Starter accounts, one Pro account, and one Enterprise account. They remove **100 + 100 + 1,000 = 1,200 units/month**. The four surviving Enterprise accounts add **2,000 units/month** through expansion. Thus:

\[
7{,}000-1{,}200+2{,}000=7{,}800\text{ units/month}.
\]

That is how revenue retention above 100% coexists with customer losses. Each customer counts once in account retention, while a large account contributes much more money to revenue retention. [B21-S1; B21-S2]

### Give overall growth a different label

Dividing all closing recurring revenue by opening recurring revenue gives **8,400 ÷ 7,000 = 120%**, or **20% growth in total contracted monthly recurring revenue**. That includes 600 units/month from acquisition. It is useful as an overall growth measure, but it is not retention of the opening cohort. Existing-cohort recurring revenue grew about **11.43%**, with acquisition providing the additional growth. [B21-S1–S3]

### What these numbers cannot explain

- **Cash collected:** annual prepayments and overdue invoices make cash timing different from monthly contract value. Their amounts are missing, so cash collections cannot be calculated from this snapshot. Obtain the receipt and invoice records. [B21-S3]
- **Revenue earned during the month:** the closing figures describe contracts at month-end. Without the effective dates of cancellations and expansions and the relevant service periods, they do not establish the month's earned revenue. Obtain dated contract changes and a revenue ledger. [B21-S2; B21-S3]
- **Profit:** higher contract value does not establish higher profit. Costs are unreported, and increased support workload does not quantify its cost. Compare earned revenue with the corresponding cost ledger. [B21-S3]
- **Reasons customers stayed or canceled:** the survey describes praise among responding active accounts. It excludes canceled accounts, and its response rate is unknown. It therefore cannot establish the views of all customers or explain cancellations. Collect response information and evidence from canceled customers as well. [B21-S3]
- **Whether cancellations stopped or growth will last:** six opening accounts canceled during this month. The packet does not establish that cancellations subsequently stopped, that expansion will recur, or that retention will persist. Follow the same account groups over later months and examine renewal and expansion patterns, especially among the large accounts driving this result. [B21-S2–S4]

A defensible dashboard would display **account retention, net retention, gross retention, new-customer recurring revenue, and total recurring-revenue growth** separately, with cash, earned revenue, and profit reported from the records that actually measure them.
