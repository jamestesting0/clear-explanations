**Both nominal deadlines can be met by scanning A first and reviewing A first.** Both jobs then finish by hour 16. The manager's 22 hours is the sum of work across different resources, not the elapsed project time: OCR can run while the scanner or reviewer does other work. [B25-S1, B25-S2]

## What the proposed changes accomplish

**An additional OCR computer does not improve these schedules under the stated model.** The existing capacity already runs both OCR jobs simultaneously without slowing either one. Another computer neither shortens A's six-hour OCR operation nor removes the scanner and reviewer constraints. [B25-S1, B25-S3]

**Expedited OCR for A does improve the nominal schedule.** With A scanned first, its OCR runs at 5–9 and its review at 9–11. B's OCR remains 8–11, followed by review at 11–14. A finishes at 11 and B at 14: both finish two hours earlier than in the ordinary schedule that meets both deadlines. The earlier A review uses time before B becomes ready. [B25-S1–B25-S3]

Expediting does not make scanner order irrelevant. With B scanned first, A's earliest nominal completion becomes 8 + 4 + 2 = 14, still after its deadline. Nor does the two-hour improvement establish that the 100-unit fee is worthwhile: the packet supplies no monetary value for earlier completion or avoided lateness against which to compare it. ## Nominal feasibility is not an actual-time guarantee

Let \(d\) be A's actual ordinary OCR duration, in hours. Even if A gets the scanner first and the reviewer as soon as its OCR finishes, its earliest completion is

\[
1+4+d+2=7+d.
\]

Meeting hour 13 therefore requires \(d\leq6\). The supplied range permits \(d=9\), when even this earliest possible path finishes at hour 16. **No ordinary-process schedule can guarantee A's deadline across the full supplied range.** [B25-S1, B25-S4]

There is no distribution from which to calculate a probability of lateness. The expedited four-hour quote supports the nominal improvement, but is not a worst-case guarantee. Excellent cluster uptime likewise supplies no bound on this file's processing duration; its layout complexity remains unexamined. [B25-S4]
