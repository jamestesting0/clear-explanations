## Calculate the threshold before applying the exception

For physical loans to an outside institution, add the insured values of loans under the **same exhibition agreement** that depart within any thirty-day interval. Review is required when that total **exceeds 50,000 units**. Borrower identity alone does not make loans related. Digital licenses contribute nothing to this calculation, and loans entirely within the network are exempt from this threshold. [B20-S1]
