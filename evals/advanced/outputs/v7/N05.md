**Opportunity cost is the value of the best alternative you give up when you make a choice.** Money already spent that you cannot recover is a **sunk cost**.

Suppose you already paid $20 for a non-refundable concert ticket. You now choose between attending the concert and taking a work shift that would earn you $60.

- The **$20 is a sunk cost**: you cannot recover it whichever option you choose.
- If the shift is your best alternative, **attending the concert has a $60 opportunity cost**: those are the earnings you give up.

Opportunity cost can also be something other than money, such as rest, study time, or time with friends. It concerns what your choice makes you forgo now.
